THE WISDOM BEHIND THE PRACTICE

Carnegie's social principles, translated into teen situations

The manuscript explicitly acknowledges Dale Carnegie's How to Win Friends and Influence People alongside Socrates and other thinkers. Chapter 1 explores how criticism can trigger self-defence. This is an adaptation of social principles rather than a translation of a single classic.

Eric Han adapts the ideas through contemporary situations and practical exercises. The excerpt below is his writing from the manuscript.
