Here's the thing nobody says out loud: the social gaps that hold teens back don't disappear at 18. They follow people into careers, marriages, and leadership roles. The adult who struggles to be heard in meetings, who avoids conflict until it explodes, who can't quite connect with the people they need — they're still running the same patterns from adolescence.

The Social Playbook works for adults too. And there's something powerful about a parent reading it alongside their teen — not just to understand what they're learning, but to close the same gaps in themselves, at the same time.

The best thing you can give your teen isn't a book. It's a parent who's applying the same principles.
