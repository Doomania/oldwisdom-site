## Repurposing recipe

For each canonical piece:

1. Publish one searchable video or article that answers the full question.
2. Cut the strongest practical step into a 20–40 second vertical clip.
3. Cut the most common parent mistake into a second vertical clip.
4. Create one Pinterest pin for the problem and one for the script or checklist.
5. Link each piece to the most relevant next guide or the quiz. Mention a Playbook only when it directly fits the topic.
6. Use a stable campaign shape: `utm_source=CHANNEL&utm_medium=organic&utm_campaign=GAP&utm_content=SLUG`.
7. After 30 days, refresh the top 20% and retire or reframe the bottom 20%. Do not mass-produce near-identical videos.

The YouTube monetisation policy excludes repetitive or mass-produced template content from monetisation. Keep every video substantively original and useful: <https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/1311392>.
