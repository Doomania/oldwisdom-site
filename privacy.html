PRIVACY POLICY

Last updated: 6 July 2026

Old Wisdom // Retold ("we", "us") respects your privacy. This policy explains what we collect, why, and your rights over your data.

Who We Are

Old Wisdom // Retold is a book series published by Eric Han, operating from Auckland, New Zealand. Contact: hello@oldwisdomretold.com.

What We Collect

  • Email address — when you sign up for a free chapter or take the quiz.
  • Quiz responses — your answers and generated profile, used to personalise your report.
  • Basic analytics — anonymised page visits and interactions, with no personal identifiers.

How We Use It

  • Deliver the free chapter and your quiz results.
  • Send occasional updates about the book series. You can unsubscribe at any time.
  • Improve the website and understand which content is useful.

Who We Share It With

We use Brevo (email service provider) to store your email and send messages. We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal data to anyone. Ever.

Data Retention

We keep your email until you unsubscribe or request deletion. Quiz data is retained only as long as needed to serve your report and improve the tool.

Your Rights

  • Unsubscribe from any email using the link in the footer.
  • Request a copy of your data, or its deletion, by emailing us.
  • New Zealand residents are protected under the Privacy Act 2020; other regions under applicable local law.

Cookies

This site uses minimal, privacy-friendly analytics that do not require tracking cookies. No advertising cookies are set.

Children

Although our books are written for teens, this website and its sign-up forms are intended for parents and adults. We do not knowingly collect data from anyone under 16 without parental involvement.

Changes

We may update this policy. The "last updated" date reflects the latest version.
