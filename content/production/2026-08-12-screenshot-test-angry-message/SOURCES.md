## Claim boundaries

- The research supports selected decision-making ideas and the named safety routes. It does not validate SCREENSHOT or promise that a message will stay private, avoid conflict, or prevent harm.
- Adult relationship research is used only to avoid a blanket “never communicate while angry” rule. It is not teen-texting evidence.
- No source supports a fixed waiting time, a diagnosis from one message, or a claim that screenshots always show the full truth.
- The no-investigation, no-unsafe-confrontation, no-dangerous-secrecy and capable-adult-lead rules are OWR safeguarding floors.
- Community language informed wording only. It is not prevalence or outcome evidence.
