**Preheader:** A three-line plan for getting into the task without forcing confidence.

Hi {{ contact.FIRSTNAME | default: "there" }},

Your teen may know the answer—and still freeze when the teacher says, “Get into groups.”

That moment asks for two things at once: schoolwork and a fast social move. By the time they work out where to sit or what to say, the louder students may have chosen the jobs.

You do not need to tell them to be more outgoing.

Try this instead: help them keep **one entry line, one visible job, and one recovery move** ready.

- **Enter:** “Have you got room for one more?”
- **Contribute:** “I think we could ___ because ___.”
- **Recover:** “I missed the start. What can I take from here?”

The goal is not to make your teen loud. It is to give them a practical way into the work—and a way back after an awkward start.
