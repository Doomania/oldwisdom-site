# Sources and claim limits

| Source | Claim-safe use | Boundary |
|---|---|---|
| McElhaney & Allen (2001), [Autonomy and Adolescent Social Functioning: The Moderating Effect of Risk](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC1551976/) | An observational, multi-informant study of 131 adolescents, mothers, and peers that supports describing adolescent autonomy as context-dependent and involving expression of viewpoints and influence within family relationships. | Older and small sample; observational associations do not show that this guide’s scripts create confidence, independence, safety, or a particular outcome. |
| [UK Government: Report child abuse](https://www.gov.uk/report-child-abuse) | Official UK service information: call 999 if a child is in immediate danger. | The 999 instruction is UK-specific. The article explicitly directs readers elsewhere to use their local emergency route; it does not present UK services as global. |

## Editorial guidance boundaries

- `NOTICE → PAUSE → CHOOSE → REVIEW` is OWR editorial guidance for ordinary, low-stakes decisions, not a clinical intervention.
- Quietness can reflect a genuine preference, processing time, compromise, uncertainty, or pressure; the article does not infer motive from one event.
- Parents retain safety, legal, and household responsibilities while involving the teen meaningfully wherever safe and appropriate.
- The article does not cover situations where a teen may be in immediate danger, and it does not promise social, educational, mental-health, or safety outcomes.
