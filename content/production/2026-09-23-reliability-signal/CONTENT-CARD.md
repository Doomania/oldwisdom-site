# Content card — reliability signal parent plan

**Date:** 2026-09-23
**Audience:** Busy parents of teens 10–16
**Status:** DRAFT_ONLY
**Slug:** reliability-signal

## Reader job
Help with ordinary missed commitments or lateness without excuses, a public parent rescue, or a promise that other people will forgive or trust the teen.

## Promise
**One visible signal, repeated.** Choose one controllable commitment, attach it to a cue and backup, and review the next few chances privately.

## Observable moment
A teen says peers, teachers, coaches, or a supervisor now see them as flaky after a pattern of ordinary inconsistent follow-through.

## Belief shift
- **From:** They need one huge apology or I need to repair the relationship for them.
- **To:** They can make a brief ownership statement and give people new information over time through one concrete action.

## Mechanism
**One-Signal Repair Streak** — observable signal; if–then cue + backup; next three relevant commitments or seven days; private factual review. OWR editorial synthesis, not a reputation-repair protocol. Research supports only the narrower cue → action element and the limit that an apology cannot compel trust; it does not test this teen plan or its social outcome.

## Required lanes
- Ordinary pattern: teen-owned plan, proportionate parent friction support.
- Policy/discipline: follow the relevant process; plan does not erase consequences.
- Persistent functioning/wellbeing change: broader trusted-adult/professional support.
- Self-harm/immediate safety: urgent local help.

## Scope lock
Not curfew trust, a one-off consequence, apology-writing, first-job onboarding, family discipline, or a promise of restored reputation.
