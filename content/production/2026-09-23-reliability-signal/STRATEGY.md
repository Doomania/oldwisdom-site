# Strategy — reliability signal parent plan

**Status:** QA_PENDING — internal draft research only. No release action.

## Reader job
Help a parent support a teen who worries they are now seen as unreliable after ordinary lateness, missed commitments, or unfinished agreed work—without writing excuses, arranging forgiveness, or promising reputation repair.

## Book fit
**War Playbook, Chapter 23: “Reputation Is Your Trailer.”** The teen-facing capability is deliberate, repeated signals rather than a grand announcement. The parent guide uses this only as an optional, teen-owned soft bridge after the complete answer.

## Mechanism
**One-Signal Repair Streak:** teen selects one observable reliability signal, practises it over a short defined period, and lets evidence—not a parent rescue—do the communicating.

## Portfolio lock
Not alarm ownership, first-job onboarding, a one-off manageable consequence, apology writing, curfew trust rules, or an adult disciplinary case. It owns an ordinary ongoing reliability pattern across a teen's commitments.
