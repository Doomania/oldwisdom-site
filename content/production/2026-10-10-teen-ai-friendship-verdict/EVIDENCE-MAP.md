## Editorial synthesis and claim boundaries
- **AI ANSWER CHECK** is OWR's unvalidated practical conversation device. No promise to prevent harm, preserve trust, prove a friend's intent, or improve relationships.
- A confident answer can be useful as a *prompt to ask better questions* while still being wrong or incomplete; product assessment supports a bounded risk, not a universal failure.
- Asking about one concrete event must serve *answer scrutiny*, not retread the published relationship-judgment checklist. Do not request a full chat transcript or publicly confront a friend based on a bot's label.
- Private-data cleanup: pause further identifiable uploads; check the particular service's settings together. Do not promise deletion from company systems.
- Ordinary disagreement is not bullying. If evidence shows online mistreatment, including a serious single threat or privacy breach, use the UNICEF cyberbullying branch; do not wait for it to become sustained. NIMH's broader child mental-health guidance supports professional consultation if emotions or behaviour last weeks, cause distress, **or** interfere with school, home, or friends. For immediate danger, local emergency help is a precautionary editorial route; WHO's emergency-care overview is not a friendship triage protocol.
- No advice to forward, screenshot, erase, or share potentially sexual/private material; safeguarding outside scope needs specialist guidance.
