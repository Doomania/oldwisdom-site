# Strategy — an AI verdict on a teen friendship

**Date:** 2026-10-10
**Status:** QA_PENDING — concept only; article publication separately gated.
**Audience:** Busy parents of teens 12–17.
**Mode:** Market-led, global-first parent guide. No claim of verified search-volume growth.

## Reader job and promise
A parent learns that a chatbot has confidently called their teen's friend manipulative. Help the parent acknowledge the teen's worry, check the *AI answer* for missing information and private-data exposure, and support a proportionate teen-owned next step without treating the label as a verdict.

**Reader moment:** At the kitchen table a teen shows a chatbot's diagnosis-like friendship answer after pasting a private exchange.
**Tension → after-state:** From choosing between dismissing the bot and accepting its verdict to asking what the tool knew, what it could not know, and what to do next in real life.
**Primary journey:** GUIDE; supporting UNDERSTAND and RELEASE.
**Mechanism:** AI ANSWER CHECK: (1) Ask what the teen hoped to understand, without demanding a transcript; (2) check what context the chatbot was given and what it cannot know about the friend's intent; (3) stop adding other people's identifiable details and pick one offline next step. Editorial conversation framework, not a validated intervention.

## Portfolio lock
Distinct from `more-friends-better-friendship-judgment` (evaluates a relationship through real interactions), `should-i-check-my-teens-phone` (parent monitoring/privacy boundary), `screenshot-test-angry-message` (before sending an angry text), `teen-friend-always-in-crisis` (peer crisis), and `read-teen-behaviour-without-guessing` (behaviour interpretation). This asset evaluates an AI-generated **answer** and the private material entered into it. It must not reproduce NOTICE → NAME → DECIDE, a generic friend-quality checklist, or generic screen-time control.
