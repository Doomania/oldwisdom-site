## Editorial synthesis and exclusion audit

**AI ANSWER CHECK** (hope → evidence/missing context → privacy and one offline step) is an OWR editorial framework, not a tested protocol. The dialogue and kitchen-table scene are illustrative. It makes no claim to repair friendships, preserve trust, improve wellbeing, or identify manipulation. We deliberately avoid a friendship-quality checklist, a phone-search/monitoring protocol, a generic screen-time guide, advice to confront the other family, and any promise of deletion from a provider's systems. Later safety instructions override ordinary “pause or do nothing” options when a serious threat, privacy breach, or immediate danger appears.
