# Short-Form Scripts

## Short 1: Why friendships die after one term

- Target length: 35-45 seconds
- CTA: free skills reflection

**Script**

[ON SCREEN: "Why teen friendships fade after one term"]

If your teen had friends last term and now everything feels cold, do not assume there was secret drama.

Often the friendship was being carried by the setting.

Same class.
Same lunch.
Same club.

Then the term changes, the routine disappears, and nobody starts the second conversation.

That is the real skill to teach.

After one decent interaction, reopen it fast:

"How did that presentation go?"
"Are you going again next week?"
"You were right, that test was brutal."

Do not wait for the room to do the work again.

## Short 2: Stop asking "Did you make friends?"

- Target length: 30-40 seconds
- CTA: free skills reflection

**Script**

[ON SCREEN: "Better question for parents"]

After a social event, stop asking:

"Did you make friends?"

That question is too big.

Ask this instead:

"What would the second conversation be?"

Because many teen friendships do not fail from rejection.
They fade because nobody reopens contact after the first easy moment.

Try:

"Will you see them again naturally?"
"What could you reference next time?"
"What is one easy follow-up?"

That turns friendship into a skill your teen can practise instead of a mystery they are supposed to solve by personality alone.
