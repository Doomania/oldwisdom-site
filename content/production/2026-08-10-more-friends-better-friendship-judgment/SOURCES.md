## Claims intentionally avoided

- No claim that friendship quantity is irrelevant, that one friend is always enough, or that popularity is inherently harmful.
- No causal claim that friendship quality alone produces wellbeing or later mental health.
- No claim that a parent’s criticism always causes defence, secrecy, or worse behaviour.
- No diagnosis of a friend as “toxic,” controlling, abusive, or dangerous from a checklist or single event.
- No claim that peer influence fully explains or excuses a teenager’s choices.
- No promise that NOTICE → NAME → DECIDE will reveal danger, secure disclosure, prevent harm, or produce a preferred decision.
- No suggestion that teen ownership overrides household rules, developmental limits, or intervention for concrete risk.
- No community post or search snippet is used as factual or representative evidence.
