# Email Draft — Why Your Teen Won’t Talk After School

**Status:** approved for provider draft preparation after live article verification; sending remains unauthorised

**Subject:** The worst time to ask, “How was school?”

**Preview:** One smaller question can work better than a doorway interview.

Your teenager walks in, drops their bag, and gives you one word.

“Fine.”

It can feel like rejection. Often, it is simply too early to know what the silence means.

The new guide gives you a four-part response:

**RESET → NOTICE → INVITE → RETURN**

Give the landing a shape. Watch the wider pattern. Ask one smaller question. Then come back when you said you would—without dropping safety checks or ordinary expectations.

Try this today:

> “Water, snack, or space first? I’ll check in after you’ve changed.”

Then later:

> “Tiring, awkward, or ordinary day?”
