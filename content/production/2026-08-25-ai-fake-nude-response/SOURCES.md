## Sources

1. **[NCMEC: Generative AI](https://ncmec.org/theissues/generative-ai)**
   **Claim-safe use:** AI can create sexualised images of children, including by classmates; NCMEC links to reporting/removal resources and says Take It Down can help when real or AI-generated explicit images circulate.
   **Boundary:** Not local legal, emergency, school, clinical, or service-routing advice.

2. **[NCMEC: Take It Down](https://takeitdown.ncmec.org/)**
   **Claim-safe use:** The live service says it hashes nude, partially nude, or sexually explicit images/videos taken of a person before 18 on-device, and says not to send, share, or download material in order to use the service.
   **Boundary:** Check live requirements; applicability and removal are not guaranteed.

3. **[Common Sense Media: Teens and Explicit Deepfakes in the Age of AI](https://www.commonsensemedia.org/research/teens-and-explicit-deepfakes-in-the-age-of-ai)**
   **Claim-safe use:** US survey of 1,314 13–17-year-olds: nearly half reported seeing AI-generated explicit material; among those, one in four said it depicted themselves or someone they knew.
   **Boundary:** US self-report survey; relevance only, not local prevalence, causation, or a claim about any family.
