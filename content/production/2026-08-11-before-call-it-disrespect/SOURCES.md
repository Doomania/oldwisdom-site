## Claims intentionally avoided

- No claim that every rude response is stress, overload, shame, or a nervous-system event.
- No claim that understanding context removes a consequence, household rule, or original responsibility.
- No diagnosis of defiance, trauma, anxiety, dysregulation, oppositional behaviour, or abuse from one interaction.
- No claim that disagreement is always respectful or that deliberate boundary-testing is merely misunderstanding.
- No promise that pausing, listening, or the framework will prevent conflict, produce disclosure, or secure repair.
- No use of community language as factual or prevalence evidence.
