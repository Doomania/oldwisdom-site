## Reader job
Help a parent support an ordinary start problem for revision, an application, or an assignment without taking ownership of daily homework or diagnosing procrastination.

## Book fit
**War Playbook, Chapter 19: “Redesign the Battlefield.”** The teen-facing capability is removing friction before the next day. The parent guide uses this only as an optional, teen-owned soft bridge after the complete answer.

## Mechanism
**Start-Runway Reset:** identify one blocker, make the first task finite and visible, pre-position materials, and move one distraction outside the launch zone. The teen chooses and performs the reset.

## Portfolio lock
Not a homework-ownership handoff, alarm-clock responsibility plan, failed-test response, AI homework policy, or teacher-help request. It owns friction in the first five minutes of one work session.
