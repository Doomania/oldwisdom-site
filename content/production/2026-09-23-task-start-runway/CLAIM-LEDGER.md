## Claims intentionally avoided

- The reset fixes procrastination, motivation, attention, executive function, grades, or performance.
- One difficult start proves laziness, anxiety, ADHD, a learning disorder, or lack of care.
- A parent should monitor homework, seize a phone, or control daily study.
- A better setup replaces disability/accommodation support.
- A US school, access, health, emergency, or crisis route applies everywhere.

## Residual claim risk

**Low to moderate, controlled.** The article turns bounded educational context into an untested household script. It labels that mechanism as editorial, does not promise outcomes, keeps academic ownership with the teen, and routes access, persistent wellbeing changes, and urgent safety concerns beyond the tool.
