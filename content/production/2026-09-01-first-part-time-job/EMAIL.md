A first part-time job can make a household busy fast: forms, shifts, rides, uniforms, and manager messages.

It can also make it tempting to take the wheel.

A better role is smaller and more useful: help your teen prepare, check the ordinary basics, then let them handle routine communication with the employer.

Our new Parent Guide gives you a short sequence:

**SIGN** — let your teen name the job and the questions they still have.

**SCAN** — check training, supervision, tasks, transport, and what to do if something feels unsafe.

**STEP BACK** — let them send ordinary manager messages and own the routine work conversation.

That is not stepping back from care. You still keep your responsibilities around safety, transport and the household. It is a way to give them real practice without leaving them alone with unfamiliar systems.
