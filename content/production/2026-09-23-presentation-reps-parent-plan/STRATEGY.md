# Strategy — presentation reps parent plan

**Status:** QA_PENDING — internal draft research only. No release action.

## Reader job
Help a parent support one ordinary upcoming presentation, audition, interview, tryout, or difficult meeting without trying to supply confidence or control selection.

## Book fit
**War Playbook, Chapter 16: “Confidence Is Preparation in Better Lighting.”** The teen-facing capability is a full run-through plus preparation for likely pressure. The parent guide will use this as an optional, teen-owned soft bridge after a complete answer.

## Mechanism
**Three-Reps Plan:** one out-loud run-through, one likely-question rehearsal, one reset line. Parent gives bounded feedback and the teen owns content and choice.

## Portfolio lock
Not quiet group-work entry/recovery, general confidence observation, first conversation, failed-test response, or school-help request. It owns preparation for one imminent evaluative moment.
