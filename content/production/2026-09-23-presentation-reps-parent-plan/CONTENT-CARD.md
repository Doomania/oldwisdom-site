# Content card — presentation reps parent plan

**Date:** 2026-09-23
**Audience:** Busy parents of teens 10–16
**Status:** DRAFT_ONLY
**Slug:** presentation-reps-parent-plan

## Reader job
Help with one ordinary imminent presentation, audition, interview, tryout, or difficult meeting without a confidence lecture or parent-controlled outcome.

## Promise
**Three small reps, then stop.** Offer one out-loud run-through, one likely question, and one reset line; the teen chooses the material and whether to use any of it.

## Observable moment
A teen says they are unprepared, wants to avoid the event, or is bracing for a coming performance moment.

## Belief shift
- **From:** My teen needs a big confidence talk or a parent-designed performance plan.
- **To:** I can offer a short practice option, preserve their ownership, and leave the outcome alone.

## Mechanism
**Three-Reps Plan** — out-loud run-through; likely-question rehearsal; reset line. OWR editorial synthesis, not a validated intervention.

## Required lanes
- Ordinary event: teen-led, brief practice.
- Access/accommodation: use agreed school/activity supports; parent may lead formal communication.
- Persistent distress/interference: broader professional/school support.
- Immediate danger/self-harm talk: urgent local help, not rehearsal.

## Scope lock
Not a social-anxiety guide, public-speaking course, peer-group participation article, or route to a grade, selection, or confidence outcome.

## Product relevance
**SOFT — War Playbook, Chapter 16.** Teen-facing preparation capability; optional only after the complete standalone guide.
