## Editorial devices not tested by sources

- the first-sentence script;
- the 60-second response;
- FEEL → FACTS → ONE CHANGE;
- all dialogue, error menus, method menus, and review timing;
- gathering three or four marked pieces; and
- the three-lane routing structure.

## Claims intentionally avoided

- A calm response, autonomy support, or one changed method will improve the next score.
- A failed test proves low ability, laziness, anxiety, ADHD, a learning disorder, or lack of care.
- The marked paper always reveals one clear cause.
- One named study method is best across subjects, ages, schools, and learners.
- Parents should stay out when access, formal support, wellbeing, or safety needs adult action.
- US education or health processes apply worldwide.

## Residual claim risk

**Low to moderate, controlled.** The article operationalises broad educational and parenting evidence into an untested household script. It says so in this ledger, uses conditional language, includes no outcome guarantee, and keeps school/access routes jurisdiction-neutral.
