# Short Video Scripts

## Short 1: One Opener Tied to the Room

- Target length: 35-45 seconds
- Audience: parents
- CTA: free skills reflection

### Script

[ON SCREEN: "Stop saying: Just talk to people."]

If your teen freezes socially, do not tell them to "just talk to people."

That advice is too vague to use.

Give them one opener tied to the room instead.

Not:
"Go make friends."

Try:
"Do we hand this in today or tomorrow?"
"Have you done this drill before?"
"Is this seat taken?"

Why does that work?

Because shared context lowers pressure.
They are not forcing a friendship. They are entering the same moment the other person is already in.

One opener. One follow-up. One clean exit.

That is a real social rep.

## Short 2: The Follow-Up Question Rule

- Target length: 40-55 seconds
- Audience: teens and parents
- CTA: free skills reflection

### Script

[ON SCREEN: "Most awkward conversations die here."]

Most awkward conversations do not fail on the first line.
They fail on the second move.

Your teen says:
"Have you done this before?"

The other person says:
"Yeah, last term."

Then your teen panics and says:
"Cool."

Instead, teach one follow-up question.

"Was it actually hard, or does it just look messy at the start?"

That works because people respond better when they feel listened to, not interviewed.

So the rule is simple:

Do not chase a clever topic.
Follow the thread they already gave you.

That is how a normal conversation starts feeling normal.
