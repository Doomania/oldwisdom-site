# Claim ledger — teen apology guide

| Article claim or action | Classification | Source / boundary |
|---|---|---|
| **OWN → ASK → REPAIR → RELEASE** | Editorial synthesis | A household coaching routine for ordinary peer harm; not a clinical treatment, legal process, school discipline policy, or validated intervention. |
| Name the teen's observable action without assigning motive | Editorial recommendation | Supports ownership without mind-reading. No outcome guarantee is made. |
| Require practical repair while leaving apology wording and delivery to the teen | Editorial recommendation | Parent authority and teen ownership are both retained. The harmed person is not required to reply, forgive, restore trust, or resume contact. |
| Immediate physical danger: move to safety and contact local emergency services | Official police guidance | NZ Police supports emergency action when someone is badly injured, in danger, or facing a serious risk to life; the number 111 is not presented as universal. |
| Immediate self-harm danger: stay with the young person and contact emergency/crisis support | Official health guidance | WHO Suicide Q&A supports not leaving a person alone when immediate self-harm danger is suspected and contacting emergency services, crisis support, health workers, or family. |
| Sexual images involving someone under 18: do not copy, download, or forward merely to report/use a removal tool; check NCMEC options | Official service guidance | NCMEC describes Take It Down, CyberTipline, and platform reporting. Live requirements and local duties must be checked. |
| Stalking needs situation-specific risk assessment and safety planning | Specialist guidance | SPARC supports the risk/safety-planning claim. The article gives no universal legal route. |
| Ongoing bullying moves to adult/school handling | Official education guidance | NZ Ministry of Education distinguishes bullying from one-off conflict and supports school reporting, support, planning, and follow-up; local routes differ. |
| No face-to-face apology or mediation for intimidation, stalking, or power imbalance | Editorial safeguarding floor | Deliberately framed as OWR's conservative floor, not a universal legal or clinical claim. |
