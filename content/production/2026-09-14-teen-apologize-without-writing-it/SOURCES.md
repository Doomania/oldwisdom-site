# Sources and claim limits

| Source | Claim-safe use | Boundary |
|---|---|---|
| [New Zealand Police, When should I call 111?](https://www.police.govt.nz/faq/when-should-i-call-111?nondesktop) | Supports emergency action when someone is badly injured, in danger, or facing a serious risk to life. | The number 111 is New Zealand-specific. The article tells readers elsewhere to use their local emergency service. |
| [World Health Organization, Suicide Q&A](https://www.who.int/news-room/questions-and-answers/item/suicide) | If someone may be in immediate danger of self-harm, do not leave them alone and contact emergency services, a crisis line, a health worker, or a capable family member. | This supports the urgent self-harm branch only. It does not assess one family or support the ordinary apology routine. |
| [NCMEC, Is Your Explicit Content Out There?](https://www.missingkids.org/gethelpnow/isyourexplicitcontentoutthere) | For sexual images or videos taken when a person was under 18, NCMEC describes CyberTipline, Take It Down, and platform-reporting options. | NCMEC is US-based, although Take It Down may be available more broadly. Readers must check the live service requirements and their local reporting duties. |
| [NCMEC, Take It Down](https://takeitdown.ncmec.org/) | The live service is the authoritative place to check eligibility and handling requirements before using the tool. | It is not a guarantee of removal from every service or location. The direct page blocked automated retrieval during this audit; the linked NCMEC explainer above was manually retrievable. |
| [SPARC, Risk & Safety](https://www.stalkingawareness.org/risk-and-safety/) | Stalking can escalate and needs situation-specific risk assessment and safety planning. | SPARC is US-based. The article does not prescribe a universal police, legal, or protection-order route. |
| [New Zealand Ministry of Education, Bullying: help and support for your child](https://www.education.govt.nz/parents-and-caregivers/schools-year-0-13/health-safety-and-wellbeing/bullying-help-and-support-your-child) | Distinguishes bullying from one-off conflict and supports asking a school for its reporting route, support, plan, and follow-up. | New Zealand-specific. Readers elsewhere are directed to their local school or education authority; no NZ process is presented as universal. |

## Editorial guidance boundaries

- **OWN → ASK → REPAIR → RELEASE** is an OWR household framework for ordinary peer harm, not a validated intervention.
- The wording and sequence are editorial recommendations; no claim is made that they cause remorse, forgiveness, reconciliation, or safer behaviour.
- The harmed person retains the right to refuse contact, forgiveness, trust, or renewed friendship.
- OWR's no-contact/no-mediation floor for intimidation, stalking, or power imbalance is a conservative safeguarding boundary, not a claim that one process is universally required by law.
- School rules, platform procedures, safeguarding duties, and local law override this routine.
