## Email

Hi,

Your teen says, “Everyone was watching. What was I supposed to do—look weak?”

This is where two common instructions fail.

“Never back down” can turn dignity into a performance for the crowd. “Just ignore it” can leave your teen without a safe destination or an adult response.

Try a third route: **CLEAR OUT**.

1. **CHECK THE LANE** — disagreement, provocation, bullying or danger?
2. **LINE ONCE** — “Stop. I’m not doing this.”
3. **EXIT TOWARD SAFETY** — move toward a named teacher, coach, driver, staffed place or agreed destination.
4. **ADULT LINK** — report who, what, where, when, witnesses and what help is needed.
5. **RECORD AND REVIEW** — keep factual notes where safe and check whether the adult plan happened.

A useful parent opening is:

> “I’m not going to call you weak for walking away, and I’m not going to leave you alone with repeated targeting. Tell me what happened before the first push or threat, who was there, and whether you could get away.”

**Immediate danger, assault in progress, a credible weapon threat, serious injury, or trapping bypasses CLEAR OUT. Get to safety where possible and contact local emergency help now. Do not delay to complete the framework.**

No script can guarantee safety or a fair response. But your family can rehearse a firm line, two destinations, one named adult and the exact report before the next hot moment.

**Read the parent guide:** https://oldwisdomretold.com/articles/stand-up-without-fighting

Standing up is not measured by how long your teen stays in the fight.
