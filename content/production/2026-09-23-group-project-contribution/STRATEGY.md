# Strategy — group project contribution parent plan

**Status:** QA_PENDING — internal draft research only. No release action.

## Reader job
Help a parent support a teen facing unequal group-project contribution without labelling peers, escalating through an angry parent email, or taking over the assignment.

## Book fit
**Social Playbook, Chapter 1: “Don’t Kick the Beehive.”** The teen-facing capability is replacing a personal criticism with a specific, usable issue. The parent guide uses this only as an optional, teen-owned soft bridge after the complete answer.

## Mechanism
**Contribution Ledger:** identify observable assigned work, the actual gap, the teen's own next contribution, then one neutral request and escalation threshold.

## Portfolio lock
Not quiet entry into group work, an individual teacher-help request, leaving group drama, an angry-message pre-send check, or apology/repair after completed harm. It owns a bounded peer-accountability issue while the school task is in progress.

Search potential is provisional; classroom-practice context and ordinary safety/access boundaries are being verified.
