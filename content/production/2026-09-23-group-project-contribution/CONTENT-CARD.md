# Content card — group project contribution parent plan

**Date:** 2026-09-23
**Audience:** Busy parents of teens 10–16
**Status:** DRAFT_ONLY
**Slug:** group-project-contribution

## Reader job
Support a teen in an unequal live group project without deciding peers are lazy, sending an angry parent email, or doing the assignment.

## Promise
**Use facts before frustration.** Record the agreed work, contribution gap, teen’s next contribution, one neutral request, and a clear school escalation threshold.

## Observable moment
A teen says they are doing all the work, another group member has not contributed, or a shared deadline is approaching without a clear division of work.

## Belief shift
- **From:** I need to pick a side, contact the school, or tell my teen to carry everyone.
- **To:** We can separate observable work from assumptions, make one clear peer request, and use the teacher route proportionately if the task needs it.

## Mechanism
**Contribution Ledger** — agreed task/role; visible contributions; current gap; teen’s next contribution; neutral request; escalation threshold. OWR editorial synthesis, not a conflict-resolution intervention.

## Required lanes
- Ordinary live task: teen-led neutral request where possible.
- Access/formal support: parent may lead established communication.
- Persistent distress/interference: broader school/wellbeing support.
- Safeguarding/immediate danger: normal report/urgent route, not ledger.

## Scope lock
Not a quiet-teen group-entry guide, a teacher-clarification article, an angry-message article, a friendship diagnosis, or a guarantee of fair contribution/marks.

## Product relevance
**SOFT — Social Playbook, Chapter 1.** Teen-facing specific-issue-not-person capability; optional only after a complete standalone answer.
