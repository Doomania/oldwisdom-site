## Editorial mechanism
**Contribution Ledger** is OWR editorial synthesis: record agreed task/role, each visible contribution, current gap, the teen’s next contribution, one neutral request, and a proportionate teacher escalation threshold. It is not evidence-tested; Edutopia supplies only classroom-practice context for clear roles/norms. It is not a way to decide who is lazy, force classmates to cooperate, or guarantee a fair mark.

## Claim controls
- Use observable task facts, not a diagnosis of peers’ motives or personality.
- Teen should lead the neutral request where able; parent may lead access/safeguarding/formal-support communication.
- Follow school process; do not ask a parent to police classmates or do the work.

## Portfolio distinction
- `quiet-teen-group-work`: entering, contributing, and recovering after a quiet start.
- `teen-wont-ask-teacher-for-help`: private request about an individual academic block.
- This guide: unequal contribution inside a live peer task, using facts and one neutral accountability step.
